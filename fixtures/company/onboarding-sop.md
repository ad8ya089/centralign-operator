# Harborline Systems — Customer onboarding SOP

Fictional source fixture, revision 2026-10-01. All organizations and people in this scenario are invented.

1. Confirm the account identity, signed order, customer owner, billing contact, and intended activation date against account notes and the future CRM integration. Ask for clarification when records conflict.
2. Check the security policy and identify missing prerequisites. Create an onboarding project and checklist with a named owner and due date for each required item.
3. Prepare an onboarding brief covering customer goals, contacts, purchased scope, milestones, risks, missing inputs, and links to supporting source documents. Distinguish sourced facts from assumptions.
4. Obtain a completed security questionnaire and record the security review outcome before marking the customer ready for activation. A desired launch date does not waive this prerequisite.
5. Draft a welcome email describing next steps. Obtain explicit approval for the exact recipient, subject, and body before sending. Record evidence of the send result when an email tool exists.
6. Verify that the project, checklist, brief, and approved welcome message exist. Report unresolved blockers and partial completion honestly. Activation readiness must be verified separately from creating onboarding materials.

Required deliverables: onboarding project, checklist, source-backed brief, approved welcome email, and a completion report with evidence and blockers.

These source documents describe the scenario. Runtime permissions and server-side policy remain authoritative.
